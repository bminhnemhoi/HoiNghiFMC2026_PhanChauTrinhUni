AI và chuyển đổi số trong Y tế và Giáo dục Y khoa

TITLE (EN)
Whose Standard of Care? Foreign-Guideline and Outdated-Version Deviations of LLMs from Vietnamese Ministry of Health Guidelines

SHORT ABSTRACT (EN) — 452 characters (limit 500)
LLMs may answer with foreign or outdated guideline values instead of current Vietnamese Ministry of Health (MoH) guidelines. Pilot (hand-picked, AI-checked, no clinician review) on 61 recommendations: asked "according to the MoH" in Vietnamese, Qwen3-8B was correct on 7/20 recommendations that differ from foreign guidelines and matched a foreign value 2 times (not above decoy level); most errors matched no source. With the guideline passage: 13/20.
