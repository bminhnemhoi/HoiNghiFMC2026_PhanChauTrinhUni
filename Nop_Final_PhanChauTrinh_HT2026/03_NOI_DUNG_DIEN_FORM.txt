[Title]  —  127/150
Whose Standard of Care? Foreign-Guideline and Outdated-Version Deviations of LLMs from Vietnamese Ministry of Health Guidelines

[Abstract field]  —  489/500
LLMs may give doses or thresholds from foreign or outdated guidelines instead of current Vietnamese Ministry of Health (MoH) guidelines. Pilot on 61 purposively selected recommendations (AI-checked only, no clinician review): asked in Vietnamese with an MoH cue, Qwen3-8B was correct on 7/20 recommendations differing from a named foreign guideline; most errors matched no recorded source; foreign matches were not shown to exceed chance (decoy) level. With the MoH passage: 13/20 correct.
