# Addendum 1 to the preregistration "Whose Standard of Care?"

**Date.** 27 September 2026.

**Status.** Written before the registration is submitted on OSF (human gate HG2.9). It is uploaded with the registration and cited in Sections 1.2, 1.3 and 6.8 of `prereg/osf_preregistration.md`. Its SHA-256 is recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. Choices marked "[pending author confirmation, …]" are defaults. The author confirms or replaces each one before submission (`review/prereg/response.md`, items D18–D26).

**Why this addendum exists.** Section 1.3 of the registration committed the author to upload a timestamped copy of the registration to the OSF project *before any pilot output was opened*. That did not happen. The pilot outputs were opened and graded, and several changes were then made or proposed because of what they showed. This addendum:

- states the facts;
- lists every change made or proposed since the last version of the registration committed before the outputs were opened;
- states the safeguards that apply to the main study.

It does not change any hypothesis, analysis set or decision rule of H1–H4.

## 3. Grader_version 1.3.0 (change C4)

**Source.** The 41 responses that the pilot grader check (row 6) classified as grader errors. As of 27 September 2026, the grader change log (`configs/grading.yaml`, `src/vnsoc/grade.py`, `src/vnsoc/normalize_vi.py`) lists these error classes:

1. **A number of another kind.** Such an answer was given label 6 (abstain). Examples: a volume or rate for a fluid-type category, a dose for a yes/no item, a count of drugs for a regimen, a systolic value alone for a blood-pressure atom. Since 1.3.0 an answer line that states such a number gets label 5 (`unit_mismatch`), for every value kind. A category answer that names a drug outside every category also gets label 5.
2. **Blood-pressure forms.** "140 mmHg / 90 mmHg" and "140 90 mmHg" were not read as one blood-pressure value.
3. **Drug lists with extra drugs.** An answer that named the key drugs of a regimen plus other drugs matched that regimen. Since 1.3.0 every drug named must belong to a recorded regimen that the answer names; otherwise the list matches no source (label 5). This applies alike to MoH, foreign, superseded and decoy regimens.
4. **Missing drug names.** Truvada, DRV/r and rifabutin were not recognised. Symmetric counterparts were added at the same time: Descovy, LPV/r and ATV/r; streptomycin and kanamycin by full name only; a common misspelling of moxifloxacin.
5. **TB drug letters written apart.** Forms such as "R H E" are now read as one code string.

**How the changes were made.** Every test case is an answer written for the test to reproduce one error type; no model output was copied (`tests/test_grade_fixes.py`, block "grader 1.3.0"). The final list of changes, the tests and the SHA-256 of the frozen grader are recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` and in the question-freeze addendum. Section 6.3 of the registration is brought in line with the frozen version before submission, or at the latest in that addendum. The pilot stays reported with grader_version 1.2.0 (row 8).

## 4. Safeguards and commitments for the main study

1. **Pilot atoms are never used to validate the grader.** Neither the pilot outputs nor main-study outputs on pilot atoms enter the validation sample of grader_version 1.3.0 or of any later version.
2. **Pilot atoms are excluded from RQ3.** They are left out of the reference, calibration and test splits of regime (a), the 500 re-randomisations, the cluster-split sensitivity analysis, the 500 regime (b) splits and the RQ3 comparators. The split procedure of registration Section 2.6 is applied to U = the frozen atoms that have a short-answer question, minus pilot atoms and minus grader-validation atoms (item 4).
3. **Sensitivity analysis B.1** (without pilot atoms and seed-table atoms) is reported next to H1 and H3 in the main results table [pending author confirmation, see review/prereg/response.md D20].
4. **Held-out grader validation before the freeze.** This is specified in registration Section 3.1, item 7, and summarised here [pending author confirmation, see review/prereg/response.md D19].
   - **Sample.** A seeded random sample of about 60 frozen non-pilot atoms, stratified by conflict status.
   - **Responses.** The greedy short-answer responses of qwen3_8b at A1 and A3 in both languages, about 240 responses.
   - **Reference labels.** The dual AI audit with adjudication gives the reference label of each response.
   - **Acceptance.** The grader is accepted if its accuracy on these responses is ≥ 0.90 and the lower limit of the two-sided 95% design-effect Clopper–Pearson interval, with atoms as clusters, is ≥ 0.85.
   - **Exclusion.** Because these atoms' main-study outputs are seen before the freeze, the validation atoms are excluded from H1–H4 and RQ3 and are reported descriptively.
5. **Pilot outputs are never pooled with main-study data.** This is unchanged. Main-study outputs for pilot atoms are regenerated from the frozen question set.
6. **Evidence under version control.** Before submission, `review/pilot_grading/` and `data/processed/` are committed, and their SHA-256 values are recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md`.
7. **Foreknowledge option.** Under "Foreknowledge of data or evidence" (registration Section 1.2), the selected option changes. The first draft selected "Authors' limited observation of the data could not influence their analysis decisions". The new selection is "Authors have observed the data, but have not performed the proposed analyses", because the pilot outputs informed the grading rules [pending author confirmation, see review/prereg/response.md D18].

They are registered in a later addendum before the question freeze, or not at all.
- **Compute plan.** The forecast from the measured laptop throughput (`review/M1/R1.3_compute.md`) exceeds the DR6 trigger at the planned atom counts. The DR6 cuts, in the registered order, are registered in the freeze addendum before any main run. Task R1.3 proposed running the five A2 signal samples on a subset only. That proposal is not adopted here because it changes the inputs of RQ3.
- **Token log-probabilities.** Whether the local runtime returns token log-probabilities, used as an RQ3 score feature, is checked before the freeze. If it does not, the feature is missing for every response and is handled by the registered missing-value rule (registration Section 5.1.7).

- **Code updates.** Four updates are still to be made:

  They are hashed in the freeze or pre-grading addendum; until then the registration text governs.
